# Turning Knowledge into Action

Overview:"Turning Knowledge into Action" (K2A) is a guidance framework developed by the Office for Health Improvement and Disparities (OHID) to enhance policy and decision-making through effective use of data and evidence. It aims to bridge the gap between data and action by presenting information in accessible formats, providing context and linking to wider knowledge sources.

## Key Principles 

* Encourages designing accessible publications tailored to different user groups  
* Supports timely delivery of relevant, clear, and targeted information  
* Promotes continuous user engagement and feedback throughout the process

The K2A Cycle: A continuous process consisting of four stages:  

* Scope: Understand user needs and define the problem  
* Plan: Identify and review solutions  
* Implement: Apply solutions and monitor effectiveness  
* Evaluate: Reflect on lessons learned and outcomes

## Core Activities

* Awareness Building  
* Stakeholder Engagement  
* Communication & Accessibility  
* Facilitating Interaction  
* Skill Building

## Using the Framework 

* Identify your audience and understand their needs  
* Clarify your purpose and main messages  
* Choose appropriate communication strategies  
* Set measurable goals to evaluate impact, including reach, usefulness and influence on policy or practice

For Teams: Use the practical checklist and the four key questions—Who, Why, What, How—to develop your Knowledge to Action plan at the start of each project, ensuring your work effectively informs decision-making and drives change.
