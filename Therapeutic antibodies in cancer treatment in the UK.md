# Therapeutic antibodies in cancer treatment in the UK

This publication provides a comprehensive overview of approved monoclonal antibody therapies used in cancer treatment within the United Kingdom. It discusses the molecular mechanisms, antigen targets, and clinical applications of these biologic agents, highlighting their role in advancing cancer care.

Key Highlights

* Development and approval of 37 monoclonal antibody products for cancer in the UK.
* Focus on antibody types including IgGs, bispecific antibodies and antibody-drug conjugates.
* Targeted therapies addressing key cancer pathways such as immune checkpoints (PD-1, PD-L1, CTLA-4), growth factor receptors (EGFR, HER2) and tumor-specific antigens (TROP2, GD2, CD19, CD20, CD33).
* Descriptions of the mechanisms of action, clinical indications and links to relevant databases for further reference.
* Emphasis on the evolving landscape of immunotherapy and targeted antibody treatments for resistant and metastatic cancers.

This review aims to inform clinicians, researchers and healthcare professionals about the current landscape of monoclonal antibody therapeutics in oncology, with insights into their molecular targets and mechanisms.
