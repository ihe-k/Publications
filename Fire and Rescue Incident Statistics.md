# Fire and Rescue Incident Statistics (YE December 2025)

This publication provides up-to-date statistics on trends in fires, casualties, false alarms and non-fire incidents attended by fire and rescue services in England. The data is updated quarterly and offers valuable insights into operational patterns and public safety.

This publication provides updated statistics on trends in fires, casualties, false alarms, and non-fire incidents attended by fire and rescue services in England. The data is refreshed quarterly, offering valuable insights into operational patterns and public safety.

Analysis Techniques:

* Data aggregation and summarisation using SQL, R and MS Excel
* Geographic mapping of incident locations using R
* Trend analysis over time

The data is organised into the following categories:

* Total Incidents Attended: Including fires, non-fire incidents, and fire false alarms.
* Fires Attended: Categorised into primary, secondary, chimney fires, and outdoor fires.
* Fire False Alarms: Differentiated by motive such as apparatus, good intent and malicious.
* Non-Fire Incidents: Including medical emergencies, road traffic collisions, flooding, water rescues and other special service incidents.
* Fire-related Fatalities and Casualties: Including fatalities, non-fatal casualties and casualties requiring hospital treatment.
* Response Times: Analyzed by incident type and severity.

For detailed insights and raw data, please refer to the official publication:  
[Fire and Rescue Incident Statistics, year ending December 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/fire-statistics)
